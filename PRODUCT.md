# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, and Vite with a static local-first delivery; hosting is deferred.

## Users

Wedding guests use the invitation and guide to learn when and where each celebration happens, how to assemble, and how to navigate between venues. The couple's families use it to share the same verified schedule and family welcome.

## Product Purpose

An invitation and practical guest guide for Pavgun Kaur and Ashmeet Singh Sohal's wedding celebrations. Success means guests can quickly find accurate event logistics while experiencing the story of the celebration and both families.

## Positioning

One continuous wedding journey pairs the six distinct ceremonies with a single verified event source that also powers guest utilities such as maps, calendars, and an itinerary.

## Operating Context

The site is shared with invitees who may be reading on a phone while planning travel between venues in Delhi, Mohali, and Zirakpur. Event dates use India time. The five supplied JPEG invitations and `WEDDING_REFERENCE.md` are the source material for names, invitation copy, and logistics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present six events in chronological order with ceremony time, assembly time, assembly point, and venue distinguished where applicable.
- Provide stable event links, directions, calendar downloads, a printable itinerary, sharing, a countdown to Anand Karaj, and an optional instrumental soundtrack.
- Use static client-side behavior; no RSVP backend, account system, or hosting setup is in this delivery.
- Keep the overlapping Sunday start times as supplied. Do not invent end times, coordinator identities, or contact numbers.
- Coordinator details stay absent until real names and phone numbers are supplied. Couple likeness illustrations stay absent until clear reference photos are supplied.
- Preserve every supplied family name and the invitation blessing. User-provided addresses take precedence over invitation print, including Crystal Resorts postal code 140601.

## Brand Commitments

Use the couple's names exactly as supplied: Pavgun Kaur and Ashmeet Singh Sohal. Preserve all bride-side and groom-side family names, the blessing, and the Punjabi ceremony names. The plan's shared visual style and six ceremony themes are surface-specific design direction recorded in the wedding implementation plan.

## Evidence on Hand

- `WEDDING_REFERENCE.md` records all names, invitation copy, addresses, event details, and source discrepancies.
- Five original WhatsApp e-invitation JPEGs remain in the project root as information references. They are not approved as public site artwork.
- No clear reference portrait photos or coordinator contacts are present yet.

## Product Principles

- Make event logistics easy to find before asking guests to explore.
- Keep every date, address, time, family name, and downloadable itinerary tied to shared wedding data.
- Preserve the human invitation and Punjabi context without putting essential logistics inside artwork.
- Keep the experience usable with keyboard navigation, reduced motion, readable contrast, and touch-friendly controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, and comfortable touch targets. Keep essential text and logistics available when images or audio are unavailable.
